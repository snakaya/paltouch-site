Disclosure and about this site

Who writes this

What to Buy in Japan is written in Japan by someone who shops in these stores. It is a guide, not a review: we describe what these shops sell and what the rules are for taking it home, and we do not claim to have personally tested every product we name.

Affiliate links

This site does not currently carry any affiliate links. Every link goes straight to the company it names and we are paid nothing for it. We do intend to use affiliate links in future; when we do, they will be disclosed on every page that carries them and they will not decide what we recommend.

What we do not do

We do not recommend Japanese over-the-counter medicines for readers to buy or import, and we do not tell readers to bring home food that their country’s customs prohibits. Customs information on this site is summarised from the official government pages we link to, and those pages are the authority, not us.

How content is made

Some content on this site is drafted with the help of AI tools and reviewed before it is published.

Privacy

This site does not use its own cookies or analytics. If you follow a link to another company's website, that site may use cookies, including to track affiliate referrals.
