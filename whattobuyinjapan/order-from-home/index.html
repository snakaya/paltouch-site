AFTER THE TRIP

How to Buy Japanese Snacks and Beauty From Abroad

Four ways to get the same items again, and what each one is actually good for.

Disclosure: The links on this page go straight to each company and we are not paid for them. If that ever changes, this notice will say so and the paid links will be marked.

The bag of snacks runs out in two weeks. Here are five ways people replace it, from easiest to most work. Which one is cheapest depends on what you are buying, not on which service is best.

RouteBest forWatch out for
Amazon and local shops The famous items: KitKat, instant coffee, well-known skincare. Prices are much higher than in Japan, and sellers vary. Check the expiry date in the listing. Stock differs a lot between the US, UK, Canadian and Australian sites.
Snack subscription boxes
(TokyoTreat, Sakuraco)		 Discovering seasonal snacks without choosing anything yourself. You do not pick the contents. Cancel before the next billing date if you only want one box.
Japanese shops that ship abroad
(Japanese Taste)		 Choosing exact products, including drugstore beauty. Shipping is charged by weight, so one larger order beats three small ones.
Proxy buying
(Buyee)		 Items no exporter carries: convenience-store limited editions, 100-yen shop goods, second-hand finds. You pay a service fee plus domestic and international shipping. Small orders often cost more in fees than in goods.
Forwarding addresses
(Rakuten Global Express)		 Shops that only deliver inside Japan. You buy on the Japanese site yourself, have it sent to a Japanese warehouse, and they forward one box to you. You still have to read the Japanese shop. Best when you are combining several orders into one parcel, because you pay international postage once.

The same customs rules apply to parcels

Shipping does not change what your country allows in. Meat-based products, including instant noodles and soup stock with pork or beef, are refused in all four countries, and the US also restricts rice. Buy candy, chocolate, senbei, tea and seasonings instead. A parcel that is turned back costs you the goods and the postage. US CBP: Prohibited and Restricted Items

How to keep the cost sane

  • Consolidate. Every route charges by parcel and by weight. One order of ten items is far cheaper than ten orders of one.
  • Decide what is actually worth it. Snacks are cheap in Japan and expensive to ship. Skincare and kitchen tools survive the maths better.
  • Check the total before you commit. Service fee, domestic shipping inside Japan, international shipping and any duty or import VAT are four separate numbers. The UK charges VAT on most parcels, Australia has a low-value threshold, and Canada adds a handling fee on top of duty.
