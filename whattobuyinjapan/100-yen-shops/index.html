100-YEN SHOPS

100-Yen Shops in Japan: Daiso, Seria and Can Do Compared

Written in Japan. Most items are 110 yen including tax, which is roughly 75 US cents at 150 yen to the dollar.

Disclosure: The links on this page go straight to each company and we are not paid for them. If that ever changes, this notice will say so and the paid links will be marked.

The base price is 100 yen plus 10 per cent consumption tax, so almost everything rings up at 110 yen. Larger stores also stock 220, 330 and 550 yen lines, clearly marked. Quality surprises visitors, and for a shopping trip this is the highest-value stop in the country.

Which chain for what

ChainStrongest atCharacter
DaisoRange and kitchen toolsThe biggest chain with the widest stock. If you only have time for one shop, make it a large Daiso. It also has the most 220–550 yen items.

Seven stationery buys that weigh almost nothing

Stationery is the category where Japan is genuinely different, and it slips into a laptop sleeve instead of eating luggage space.

  • 0.38 mm gel pens. The fine tip is standard here and unusual elsewhere. Sold singly and in sets.
  • Erasable gel pens. Widely available; the refills are the cheap part.
  • Sticky-note tabs in translucent film, for marking pages without damaging them.
  • Washi tape. Flat, light, and the patterns rotate seasonally.
  • Slim notebooks with grid or dot rulings.
  • Clear folders with regional or character prints. They also protect receipts and boarding passes.
  • Mechanical pencil leads in 0.3 and 0.5 mm.

What to leave on the shelf

Suitcase space runs out faster than money does, so the honest answer is that some of the best items are the ones not to buy.

  • Ceramics and glassware. Heavy, easy to chip, and a poor use of a kilo. Mugs and bowls are the classic mistake.
  • Liquids. Cleaning products and detergents leak in a suitcase and some cannot fly at all.
  • Anything you already own. Folding boxes and storage bins are excellent and also sold everywhere at home.
  • Aerosols. Pressurised cans are limited by airlines. Check before you buy.
